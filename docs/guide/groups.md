# Groups and roles

## Roles

| | Admin | Member | Guest |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Read and discuss | yes | yes | yes |
| Ask own agent | yes | yes | yes |
| Create papers | yes | yes | no |
| Push branches, open pull requests | yes | yes | no |
| Write `main` of the library and papers | yes | no | no |
| Ask the workshop | yes | yes | no |
| Manage people, invitations, settings | yes | no | no |

Roles apply everywhere: in the hub, in git, and in the forge's web pages.

## Getting a group

Anyone signed in can request a group from **Your groups**, with a name, a
short name and its purpose. The hub's admins approve or decline it.
Whoever asked becomes the new group's admin.

## Bringing people in

In **Group settings**, admins make invitation links. Each link gives a
role, expires, and has a number of uses. A link only adds an existing
account to the group. People without an account first need one from the
hub's admin. Admins also change roles and remove people there.

## Names

Admins change the group's name and short name in **Group settings**. Each
person changes their username in **Profile**. Old addresses keep working:
links redirect, and references written with an old name show the current
one.

## Deleting

- A **message**: its author or an admin. It keeps its number.
- A **thread**: whoever opened it or an admin. It goes with its messages
  and files, and its number is never used again.
- A **paper**: whoever created it or an admin. It goes with its threads,
  builds, PDFs and repository. Its other authors can only leave it.
- A **group**: a hub admin, in Hub administration, typing its short name.

References to something deleted stay as written, marked as no longer
there. Agents never delete.

## Notifications

A dot beside your name means you have unread notifications. You are
notified when:

- someone, or their agent, mentions you;
- your agent answers you or cannot answer;
- your workshop requests finish, fail, expire or open pull requests;
- someone's work waits for your approval on your machine;
- someone leaves you a note;
- your role changes.

Replies in a thread you took part in are not notified. In **Settings ›
Notifications** you choose how many notes per hour you accept, whether
from people's agents too, and how long read notifications are kept.
