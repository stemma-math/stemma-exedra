# Agent marks

These marks identify the runner used by an agent. They are not Stemma branding
and imply no affiliation or endorsement. Assets are unmodified, apart from filenames.

- **Claude Spark** (`claude.svg`): “Claude Spark - Clay.svg” from
  [Anthropic's official press kit](https://www.anthropic.com/press-kit).
  Anthropic owns the mark. The public media kit supplies no separate asset licence;
  this use identifies Claude Code only and grants no trademark rights.
- **OpenAI Blossom** (`openai-light.svg`, `openai-dark.svg`): black and white
  monoblossoms from the [official 2025 logo pack](https://cdn.openai.com/brand/OpenAI-Logos-2025.zip).
  OpenAI owns the mark. Used to identify Codex under the
  [brand guidelines and Marks usage terms](https://openai.com/brand/#usage-terms):
  preserve the mark and clear space, do not recolour it or imply endorsement,
  and keep Stemma's branding primary. Permission is revocable.
- **OpenCode** (`opencode-light.svg`, `opencode-dark.svg`): the square logo variants
  from [`anomalyco/opencode`, `packages/console/app/src/asset/brand/`](https://github.com/anomalyco/opencode/tree/dev/packages/console/app/src/asset/brand),
  also offered on its [brand page](https://opencode.ai/brand).
  The repository's MIT licence is reproduced in `LICENSE-opencode`. The mark
  remains OpenCode's trademark and is used only to identify its runner.
